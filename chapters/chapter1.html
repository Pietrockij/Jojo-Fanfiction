Chapter 1 | Reunion

Above the bustling concourse of Leonardo da Vinci–Fiumicino Airport, the split-flap arrival board clicked and clattered in a steady, mechanical rhythm, cycling through international itineraries under the warm Mediterranean glare.

11:18   PARIS   ARRIVED
12:03   TOKYO   ARRIVED

Outside, the landing gear of an Alitalia jet screamed against the tarmac, leaving a skid of burning rubber before decelerating down the runway.

Among the crowd streaming through the terminal gates stood a towering figure who commanded the space around him without saying a single word. Clad in a long dark coat that fluttered with every deliberate stride, his signature peaked cap pulled low over sharp, piercing eyes, Jotaro Kujo drew glances from tourists and airport staff alike.

Near the exit partition, a man in a crisp charcoal suit held up a stark white placard bearing bold black lettering:

KUJO JOTARO

A discreet brass pin in the shape of the Speedwagon Foundation emblem glinted on the lapel of the man's jacket.

"Mister Jotaro," the Foundation agent greeted him with a respectful bow. "It is an honor to have you in Rome."

Jotaro adjusted the brim of his cap, his teal eyes scanning the bustling terminal. "Where is he?"

"His flight from Paris landed an hour ahead of schedule, sir," the employee explained, gesturing toward the private transport waiting curbside. "We have already escorted him to the hotel."

Meanwhile, across the historic heart of Rome, Jean Pierre Polnareff was locked in a desperate, high-stakes battle. It was not against an enemy Stand user. It was against an ancient, merciless foe: European plumbing.

Inside the tiled bathroom of his hotel room, Polnareff hovered precariously above a porcelain basin sunk directly flush into the floorboards—a squat toilet. His knees shook from the strain of holding a millimeter-perfect crouch, his fingers clawing the air as he tried with all his might not to let a single thread of his clothes make contact with the damp, unfamiliar floor.

A solitary tear formed in the corner of his eye.

"Dammit all to hell!" Polnareff hissed through gritted teeth, trembling with sheer outrage. "I thought the Speedwagon Foundation was practically swimming in billions of dollars! All that oil money, and they can't even book a room with a civilized, Western-style toilet?!"

Rap, rap, rap.

A sharp knock rattled the bathroom door, nearly causing Polnareff to lose his balance and plummet into catastrophe.

"Gah! What is it?!" he barked.

"Monsieur Polnareff?" came a muffled voice from the hallway. "Mister Jotaro has arrived."

Polnareff threw the door open, wiping sweat from his brow. "Where is he?!"

In the quiet, marble-lined foyer of the hotel, Jotaro stood beside a low mahogany table. Framed in glass on the wall was a familiar photograph, one taken beneath the merciless desert sun of Egypt. Five men and a stray dog, shoulder-to-shoulder, gazing toward destiny. Jotaro lingered on the faces of old comrades, a faint, almost imperceptible smirk breaking his usually stoic expression.

"Jotaro!"

The familiar, boisterous voice echoed across the marble floor. Polnareff hurried down the stairs, waving a hand in greeting.

Jotaro turned, casually sliding both hands into his pockets. "Yare yare daze... Good grief. It's good to see you, Polnareff."

Polnareff pulled up a chair and dropped into it with a sigh of relief, leaning forward with sudden seriousness. "So, let's hear it. What's this urgent matter that dragged us all the way to Italy?"

Jotaro's expression hardened, returning to business. "The Speedwagon Foundation tracked down someone who claims to know the truth behind the origins of Stands. And he's willing to talk."

Reaching into his coat, Jotaro produced a glossy surveillance photograph and slid it across the glass table.

Polnareff picked it up, squinting at the sharp-featured man pictured under harsh fluorescent light. "And who is this shady-looking guy?"

"His name is D'oliva Olio," Jotaro replied calmly. "An ex-member of Passione, the Italian mafia."

Polnareff's eyebrows shot up. "A mafioso? You expect us to put our faith in the word of a gangster? Call me skeptical, Jotaro."

"I don't like it any more than you do," Jotaro said, his tone unwavering. "But it's our only solid lead. If we want to understand where these powers truly come from, we don't have the luxury of picking who gives us the answers."

Jotaro glanced down at his wristwatch. The second hand swept with relentless precision.

"Right now, he's being held here in Rome. In the prison of Regina Coeli."

Jotaro pushed back his chair and stood up, his towering silhouette casting a long shadow across the lobby floor.

A few hours later, Rome, Italy...

Under the darkening Roman sky, the grim, fortress-like silhouette of Regina Coeli prison loomed over the banks of the Tiber. Its weathered stone facade and iron-barred windows radiated the cold, suffocating weight of history and confinement.

Jotaro pulled down the brim of his cap, his dark coat billowing gently in the evening breeze.

"Let's hurry," Jotaro said, his deep baritone cutting through the quiet street. "We don't want to linger in a place like this."

Polnareff matched his stride, though his brow was knitted in obvious skepticism. "Wait a minute, Jotaro. If this guy really is an ex-mafioso, how are we even supposed to talk to him safely?"

Jotaro didn't break his stride. "We aren't."

"Eh?"

"We're here to break him out."

Polnareff nearly tripped over his own feet, his jaw dropping in sheer disbelief. "What?!"

"It was his only condition," Jotaro replied coldly, keeping his gaze locked on the fortified perimeter. "Deal with it."

"You didn't say a damn word about a prison break back at the hotel!" Polnareff hissed frantically, waving his hands in exasperation.

Before Polnareff could voice another complaint, the heavy steel security door creaked open. A stern female prison guard stepped out onto the threshold, her eyes narrowing as she spotted the two towering foreigners marching toward the restricted entrance.

"Hey, you bastards!" she barked in coarse Italian, her hand dropping instantly to the heavy baton at her hip. "What the hell do you think you're doing here?! Get the fuck away from the gate!"

Jotaro halted just paces from the barrier, his expression stone-cold. "Sorry, lady. But we need to enter."

"Don't take another step, or I'll beat your ass into the pavement!" she snarled, drawing her baton and raising it high.

Jotaro met her furious glare with absolute indifference. Then, he took a single, deliberate step forward.

"I warned you!"

With a vicious snap, the guard swung the heavy baton straight toward his temple. In that razor-thin fraction of a second, Jotaro's lips moved with quiet precision.

"STAR PLATINUM: THE WORLD!"

A deafening acoustic distortion tore through reality. Colors inverted into negative shades, and the flow of time ground to a dead halt. The baton hung frozen inches from where Jotaro had stood, dust motes suspended in midair like specks of glass.

Within the halted world, Jotaro casually stepped past her outstretched arm, taking his place directly behind her back.

Time resumed with a violent rush of displaced air.

SWOOSH!

The baton cleaved through empty space, throwing the guard off balance. She blinked in utter bewilderment. The towering Japanese youth had vanished into thin air.

"Like I said..." a deep, chilling voice rumbled directly behind her ear, "...let us in."

The guard stiffened. Cold sweat broke out across her forehead as she turned slowly, looking up into Jotaro's towering, menacing figure. Every survival instinct in her body screamed that she was standing before an impossible predator. Paralyzed with raw terror, she let her weapon slip from trembling fingers.

"Let's go," Jotaro said flatly, pushing past the checkpoint.

Polnareff scrambled after him, accidentally bumping his shoulder against the paralyzed guard as he squeezed through the doorway. "Pardon, mademoiselle!"

Inside, Regina Coeli was a labyrinth of echoing stone, flickering fluorescent lights, and endless tiers of barred iron doors. The heavy scent of damp concrete and rust hung thick in the stale air.

Polnareff looked up and down the seemingly infinite hallways, scratching his silver flat-top in frustration. "There's so many cells in this place! How are we supposed to reach him?"

"Simple," Jotaro said. "Let's ask a guard."

Just down the passage, two uniformed guards stood talking near a station desk.

"Hey, you!" Jotaro called out.

One of the guards blinked, pointing a thumb at his own chest in confusion. "Me?"

"Yes, you," Jotaro replied, his shadow swallowing the corridor. "We're looking for D'oliva Olio."

Intimidated by the stranger's sheer size and commanding presence, the first guard instinctively pointed toward the east wing. "H-He's down in that hallway..."

As Jotaro and Polnareff strode past, the second guard nudged his partner, muttering with furrowed brows: "Wait... Olio has another visitor?"

"Like always," the first guard whispered, quickly turning his back. "Better not get involved with him."

"Over here, Jotaro!" Polnareff called out from the end of the wing.

Inside a secluded cell, sitting leisurely at a small wooden table, was a sharp-featured man with slicked back hair. Spread across the table was a full board of Risk. He was playing the strategy game entirely against himself, chuckling with theatrical delight as he rattled a pair of red dice in his palm.

"Ah!" Olio looked up through the iron bars, a smug, toothy grin spreading across his face. "My saviors are finally here!"

Jotaro approached the cell, his eyes as cold as glacial ice. "We are not your saviors. One wrong move, and you'll be dead."

Olio merely leaned back in his chair, folding his hands behind his head with an arrogant laugh. "I'm afraid you can't kill me, though. I'm the only one who knows about the origin of Stands."

"Shut up," Jotaro grunted.

Polnareff leaned in close, whispering out of the corner of his mouth: "...He is right, Jotaro."

Jotaro's eyes narrowed. In an instant, a towering, purple-skinned warrior manifested at his shoulder, its golden armor and flowing black hair radiating sheer, overwhelming spiritual pressure.

STAR PLATINUM!

With a thunderous, split-second roar of kinetic force—

CRACK!

Star Platinum's fist smashed into the heavy iron gate. The reinforced steel bars shattered like brittle twigs, twisting and tearing away from the masonry in a violent spray of sparks and pulverized mortar.

Olio shielded his eyes from the shockwave, then dusted off his prison uniform with an amused smirk. "Mamma mia... you are so humane!"

WHEEE-OOO! WHEEE-OOO!

High above, emergency red klaxons began spinning along the ceiling, bathing the corridor in blaring sirens and flashing crimson light.

"The alarm!" Polnareff shouted. "Let's hurry up!"

"Run," Jotaro commanded.

The trio sprinted through the corridors, their footsteps pounding against the stone floor. As they rounded the corner toward the main gate, they blew past the two guards from earlier.

"Wait... isn't that Olio?!" Guard 1 stammered, reaching for his belt.

Guard 2 grabbed his partner's arm, shaking his head vigorously. "Like I said, let's not get involved!"

They burst into the central foyer, the way to the outside world just ahead.

"Wait right there!"

A booming, authoritative voice rang out, echoing over the wailing sirens. Jotaro, Polnareff, and Olio ground to an immediate halt.

Stepping out from the shadows of the exit archway was a formidable figure clad in the pristine, dark-blue dress uniform of the Italian State Police. Gold braid gleamed on his shoulders, his chest adorned with medals of distinction. A peaked officer's cap cast a dark shadow over his stern, unyielding features, and pinned over his breast was the polished gold shield of a Police Chief.

An ominous, crackling aura of supernatural energy shimmered like heat haze around his silhouette.

The Police Chief rested a gloved hand on his hip, his sharp gaze locking onto the intruders with lethal intent.

"Take the prisoner back to his cell," the Police Chief commanded, his voice dripping with venomous authority, "...or I'll attack you with my Stand!"

Jotaro and Polnareff's eyes widened in unison.

"What?!"
